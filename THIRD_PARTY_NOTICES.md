# Third-party components

- [clashctl](https://github.com/nelvko/clashctl), copyright 2024–2026 nelvko, MIT license. The installer fetches commit `4ec42a9f33108a16281bdad4eace7ccce94ae3b2` from the upstream repository. Its source and license stay in the separate clashctl installation. Existing installations are reused.
- [Mihomo](https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo), GPL-3.0, downloaded and managed by clashctl as a separate executable. This repository does not bundle its binaries.
- [yq](https://github.com/mikefarah/yq), MIT license, downloaded and managed by clashctl.
- GTK, PyGObject and WebKitGTK are provided by the operating system packages under their respective licenses.
- [FlClash](https://github.com/chen08209/FlClash) is the visual and interaction reference. The application code, CSS and icon artwork here were independently implemented; FlClash source, binaries and artwork are not bundled.

The MIT license in this repository applies to Clashctl Desktop's own code. It does not replace the licenses of these components.
